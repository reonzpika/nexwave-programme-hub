Products

Inbox Helper
Reads every incoming inbox item (labs, radiology, discharge summaries, specialist letters, portal messages) and assigns an urgency level, so the GP works the queue by what needs attention, not arrival order.
Target: ≥90% triage accuracy · ≥99% sensitivity on critical findings
Care Gap Finder
Continuously scans the enrolled register and surfaces patients overdue for diabetes, CVD-risk, and other key checks, as they fall due rather than only when a nurse runs a report.
Target: ≥95% CVDRA accuracy
