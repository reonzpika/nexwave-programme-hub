Why this exists

NexWave Health builds NZ-owned clinical AI for general practice. The founder is a practising GP who also writes the code. The build partner is Medtech, whose practice-management software runs in roughly 60% of NZ general practices.

Inbox Helper

Reads every incoming lab result, radiology report, discharge summary, specialist letter and portal message, and flags how urgent each one is. The GP works the queue by what matters, not by what arrived first.

Care Gap Finder

Continuously scans the enrolled patient list for people overdue for a diabetes, cardiovascular-risk or other key check, and surfaces them as they fall due, not only when someone runs a report.

Both tools only surface information. The GP makes every clinical decision. Neither tool ever decides or acts on its own.

Why this needs R&D, not just building: clinical AI can't be shipped on faith. Before either tool can be trusted in a real practice, its accuracy and safety have to be proven properly, on real and synthetic New Zealand data. That proving-out is the R&D.

The grant, in one line: MBIE (the government agency that funds research and innovation) is co-funding two years of this work, 12 March 2026 to 11 March 2028, worth $286,770 of a $716,926 total project. In return, MBIE expects the programme to be run and reported properly: tracked budgets, risk registers, quarterly claims. Building that discipline is Capability Development, and it's the reason your role exists.

How the work is organised

Every piece of work in the programme sits in one of these three. Knowing which one you're looking at tells you who owns it and what "done" means.

Domain 1R&D

The actual research. Choosing the technical approach, running experiments, proving the tools are accurate and safe enough for a real GP to rely on.

Domain 2Capability Development

Building the organisational skills to run R&D properly, alongside the science itself: regulatory and legal know-how, tracking research information, and project management. Three funded streams, $36,000 total. One stream exists to build your capability

Domain 3Programme Operations

The admin machine that keeps the grant alive: quarterly claims back from MBIE, payroll, contracts, and the MBIE relationship itself.

Objectives and streams

The programme is eight funded objectives: four research, four capability. Open any one for what it actually means and where it stands right now.

R&D objectives
Foundation AI architecture and data requirementsIn progress

Mar – Jun 2026

Pick the technical architecture and define the NZ data needed. Synthetic-data prototypes need to hit the accuracy targets, and the Medtech and Indici test environments need to be connected.

Where it stands: architecture drafted, currently waiting on a labelled dataset and GP clinical review before the final choice is confirmed.

Real-world data handling for inbox managementOn-track

Apr – Dec 2026

Make the chosen architecture handle messy real inbox data safely, with confidence calibration and zero unsafe automatic actions.

Where it stands: early window, builds directly on Objective 1's architecture choice.

Clinical accuracy validation for care gap detectionNot started

Jul 2026 – Apr 2027

Get CVD and diabetes risk calculations right in edge cases, and check the tool doesn't introduce new bias across different patient groups.

Where it stands: not yet begun, sequenced after Objectives 1 and 2.

Performance validation and real-world deploymentNot started

Apr 2027 – Jan 2028

Measure the system across 10 to 20 real practices against clinical, safety and operational targets, then write the final R&D report.

Where it stands: not yet begun, the last objective in the sequence.

What to watch for

The deepest layer. This is the part that's actually your job.

Not reading everything: noticing when one of these four things is true, and telling Ryo.

This is also the exact list Ryo is bringing into Wednesday's session with Mark, so the session has real material to design against instead of a blank page.

Task staleness

A task hasn't moved in a while: no updates, no progress, still sitting open past when it should have closed.

Overdue gates

A date has already passed: a decision that was due, a deadline that slipped without anyone flagging it.

Vendor silence

An external contact was chased and hasn't replied within a reasonable window, usually around a week.

Disclosure obligations

Something needs to be told to MBIE, a change, a delay, a risk, before it quietly becomes a compliance problem.
